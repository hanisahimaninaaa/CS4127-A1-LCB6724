Plan Your Visit

Find out information on how to travel to Brunei and what to do when you landed!

01 Getting to Brunei Darussalam

Brunei International Airport (BWN) the main landing with Royal Brunei Airlines, our local airlines. Schedule a ticket through their website!

Book a ticket

02 Travel around Brunei

Visitors can book for a taxi, bus or use Dart to get around as car transport is the main transportation. The application is available for both Android and iOS.

Install Dart app

03 Visa guidance

Visa requirement vary depending on your nationality, passport type, and purpose of travel. Check the recent news on visa guidance through Brunei's Immigration or Ministry of Foreign Affairs' website.
